## 1. Scope basis

The mission target is Malaysian children aged 6–7 preparing for Year 1 (KSSR). The content
plan was confirmed against publicly available descriptions of the Malaysian Year 1
mathematics scope. The resulting scope centres on:

- Whole numbers to 20 (counting, reading, writing, ordering)
- Number bonds to 10; addition and subtraction within 20 (then readiness toward 100)
- Ordinals, even/odd exposure within 20
- 2-D and 3-D shapes, position and direction
- Length/size comparison, daily time, simple data handling
